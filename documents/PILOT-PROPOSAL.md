## Problem statement

Many group home programs face the same operational challenges:

- Multiple staff members share responsibility for the same clients; documentation must be **continuous**, not duplicated across shifts.
- Supervisors need **same-day visibility** into which reports are missing or still in draft, **by home**.
- On-site staff should only work with **clients at the home where they are physically located**, to reduce errors.
- Documentation must support **internal supervision** and **funder oversight** without redundant data entry.

---

## Proposed solution

A managed, secure web application ([reports.agencyname.ca] or similar) that provides:

- **One shared daily report per client** across morning, afternoon, and evening shifts
- **Draft and submitted** workflow with admin oversight
- **Multi-facility structure** (homes, clients, staff roles)
- **Optional on-site geofencing** so employees only see clients for the facility they are inside
- **Location Help** for facility staff and a **Location requests** queue for administrators when laptops cannot detect the correct site

## Scope of the pilot

### Included

**Software (managed instance)**

- Branded web application URL for [Agency Name]
- Facility and client setup, including import assistance from your roster
- Daily reports with shift-based sections and file uploads (appointments, BIR, AWOL, injuries where applicable)
- Admin dashboard for in-progress and missing reports, filterable by facility
- Staff accounts with role-based access (administrator and employee)
- On-site geofencing configuration per home (address, coordinates, and/or map pin)
- Location Help page for employees; administrators receive location reports when staff need support

**Onboarding and support**

- Kickoff meeting and system configuration (Week 1)
- Staff training session, approximately 45–60 minutes (recorded for future staff)
- Weekly check-in calls (Weeks 2–4), then biweekly through Week 12
- Geofence and location troubleshooting (response within [48] business hours after initial tuning)
- End-of-pilot metrics review and data export
- Written summary of pilot results and renewal recommendation

### Not included in the pilot

The following may be scoped separately after a successful pilot:

- Custom ministry or funder PDF templates
- Integrations with payroll, scheduling, or government portals
- Native mobile applications
- Full electronic medication administration (eMAR) or clinical EHR functionality
- Custom report field builder per agency

## Success criteria

Success will be measured at the end of the 90-day pilot period:

1. **Completion rate:** At least 80% of active clients have a **submitted** report on at least 80% of operational days (operational day runs 6:00 AM to 6:00 AM).
2. **Oversight:** The designated program lead can identify **missing reports by home** without contacting each site individually.
3. **User satisfaction:** At least two supervisors or managers rate the system as **preferable or equal** to the previous process (short structured survey).
4. **Operational stability:** Location and geofence issues are addressed within the agreed support SLA after the initial two-week tuning period.

If these criteria are met, [Agency Name] and [Your Company] will discuss transition to an **annual subscription** (see Investment).
