## When each option fits

### Excel or paper

**Best for:** A single home, few clients, one person who maintains the spreadsheet reliably.

**Breaks down when:**

- Multiple homes operate under one agency
- Shifts hand off care for the same clients
- Supervisors need to know what is missing before end of shift
- Funders or internal audit ask for consistent, searchable records

**Hidden costs:** Coordinator time chasing staff, version conflicts, no geofence or role-based access, weak accountability.

### Enterprise residential / care platform

**Best for:** Large operators, medication-heavy programs, dedicated IT, long implementation budgets.

**Less ideal when:**

- Organization runs 4–12 bed group homes with simple daily documentation needs
- Budget is constrained (government-subsidized rates)
- Timeline must be weeks, not 12–18 months

**Trade-off:** Deep functionality vs. cost, complexity, and training.

### Our platform

**Best for:**

- Nonprofit or private operator with **2+ homes** and **15–80+ clients**
- Must document **daily** for internal supervision and funder accountability
- Limited IT staff; needs something that **works on facility laptops in Chrome**
- Wants **supervisor dashboard** without building spreadsheets

**Honest limitations:**

- Not a replacement for full eMAR or hospital-grade clinical records
- Standard daily report template in pilot; custom funder forms are a separate project
- Geofencing depends on browser location quality (we provide tools and support to tune it)

## Objection handling

| Prospect says | Suggested response |
|---------------|-------------------|
| “We already use Excel.” | “Excel works until you have multiple homes and overnight staff—then no one knows what’s missing. We show gaps by home the same day, with one report per client.” |
| “Is our data safe?” | “Data is hosted on [provider/region]. Access is role-based. You own your data, and we provide export on exit. Here is our privacy one-pager.” |
| “Staff won’t adopt new software.” | “We run a 90-day pilot with training and on-site location support. Success is measured at 80% completion, not perfection on day one.” |
| “Does this meet ministry requirements?” | “The pilot uses our daily log built for group homes. If a specific ministry PDF is required, we scope that as phase two—many agencies run our log internally first.” |
| “Location doesn’t work on laptops.” | “That’s common on facility Wi‑Fi. We built Location Help for staff and location requests for admins—we tune geofence with you during onboarding.” |
| “Why not buy [big vendor]?” | “If you need full eMAR and enterprise integrations, they may fit. If you need daily logs and multi-site oversight quickly and affordably, we’re built for that niche.” |
